BEACHES TURKS & CAICOS CALORIE ESTIMATES
Prepared October 26, 2026

Purpose and coverage
An unofficial calorie comparison layer for the existing Beaches Menu Book. The
533 records include individual dishes, duplicate lunch/dinner listings, guest
reports, and some grouped choices. They are not 533 unique dishes. Buffet signs
without named dishes are outside the row inventory and have no fixed estimate.
No official Beaches calorie declaration was used. The menu inventory was not
comprehensively refreshed; historical dates and guest-source qualifications remain.

What Grade D means
Every numeric estimate is Grade D, "Use with caution": ingredient descriptions
plus hypothetical portions and generic food proxies. The grade describes evidence
quality, not whether a food is healthy. No resort food was weighed, recipes are
not disclosed, and neither pictures nor AI illustrations establish mass/nutrition.
No A/B/C upgrade is made from an item name, photo, menu weight or source count.

Arithmetic and ranges
For each component, kcal = edible grams * source kcal per 100 g / 100.
Central estimates sum the central component amounts. Lower and upper scenarios
sum the corresponding low/high component amounts. Unless a component has an
explicitly specified bound, the scenario weights are 65%, 100%, and 150% of the
assumed central amount. These are sensitivity scenarios chosen by the modeler,
not measurements, empirical restaurant-portion bounds, probability distributions,
or confidence intervals. Actual portions/composition may fall outside them.
The scenarios vary quantities; they do not prove the chosen proxy or recipe.
Central display values round to the nearest 10 kcal; lower values round down and
upper values round up. Very small central values display as <10 kcal, not zero.
All unrounded arithmetic and component weights are retained in estimates.json.

USDA source data
USDA FoodData Central, FNDDS 2021-2023 (October 2024 release):
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400530/pdf/fndds/2021_2023_FNDDS_Doc.pdf
The complete dataset already existed locally from the Trash Can Tables work.
profiles.json preserves the exact selected FDC IDs, descriptions, calories per
100 edible grams, provenance URLs and source-file SHA-256. Rebuilding uses these
retained profiles and does not require that large local dataset.

Cooked conch is not present in that FNDDS release. Its profile is from USDA SR28
(2015), NDB 15250/FDC 173720, 130 kcal per 100 g, page 678 of the official report:
https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400535/Data/SR/SR28/reports/sr28fg15.pdf#page=678
This is a cooked-conch proxy even for citrus-marinated conch, not a measured raw
conch value. Fried preparations model coating/oil or a clearly identified proxy.

Proxy limitations
Food profiles are approximate matches, not Beaches recipes. Examples include
generic white fish for mahi/grouper/corvina, sea bass for branzino, bulgur for farro,
mashed potato for cassava/parsnip puree, meatless links for plant-based chorizo,
ricotta for mascarpone, cooked pasta for rice/soba noodles, and prepared pastry,
cake, pudding or pie for specialty desserts. Relevant differences are recorded
in item notes and the exact proxy names are visible. Some prepared USDA foods
already contain cooking fat. Added components represent named accompaniments or
explicitly assumed extra fat; the model does not establish actual retained oil.
Ingredient substitutions, food hydration and richer recipes can change calories.
No separate macro, sodium, allergen or daily calorie target is inferred.

Serving decisions
Pizza: one assumed whole individual pizza, generally around 350 g centrally;
diameter and actual weight unknown. It is not per slice.
Soy rolls: four pieces as listed. Nigiri/sashimi alternatives remain unavailable.
Butch's 14 oz bone-in Kansas strip for two: half the estimated cooked edible meat
per person, with assumed bone/cooking loss. Side/sauce choices excluded.
Other labeled steak ounces: weight basis is unknown; cooked edible weights span
assumed pre-cook/cooked interpretations. Bones are excluded from edible weights.
Children's meals use explicit smaller assumptions, not adult meal estimates.
Kimonos proteins, rice, soup and starters are separate components of a meal.
Optional add-on protein and selectable fries/salad are excluded where the serving
line says so. Named fixed accompaniments are included. Reorders/refills not modeled.
Generic side/sauce lists and unnamed buffets do not receive a universal number.

Reproducibility
scripts/nutrition_models.py contains one explicit model/withholding decision for
every pinned inventory row. No fuzzy or keyword matching assigns nutrition.
scripts/menu_inventory.cjs reads the actual page data. The builder refuses a menu
snapshot mismatch. Rebuild: python3 scripts/build_nutrition.py
Byte-for-byte freshness: python3 scripts/build_nutrition.py --check
Domain checks: python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
Browser checks: node scripts/check_nutrition.cjs (Playwright required).
Passing these checks establishes software consistency, not nutrition accuracy.
