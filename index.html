Before you book a table

Reservations, dress code, ages, allergies, extra costs

Book Kimonos and Butch’s

They are the only two the resort marks reservation-required, both at dinner. Book in the Beaches app or at the Culinary Concierge desk; Kimonos fills fast. Some guides add Le Petit Chateau and Sapodilla’s, so check the app on day one.

Pack one dinner outfit

Evening attire at Sky, Sapodilla’s, Le Petit Chateau, and Butch’s at dinner. A travel-agent guide puts that at long pants and a collared shirt for men, no beachwear.

Three restaurants are 12 and up

Sky, Sapodilla’s and Le Petit Chateau, per the resort.

Allergies and special diets

Register them at the Culinary Concierge desk (central, open days). Special preparations or products the resort doesn’t stock, kosher included, need a month’s notice before arrival. The menus state the kitchens are not allergen-free.

A few things cost extra

Food and standard drinks are included. Butch’s and Pinta print prices on premium wine, and guides report paid wine lists at Sky, Sapodilla’s and Le Petit Chateau.

Menus drift

The posted PDFs range from 2020 to 2026, and each menu here shows its date. At check-in you get a sheet with hours and a QR code for each restaurant’s current menu.

How calorie estimates work

Prepared October 26, 2026 for the existing menu-book inventory. All numeric estimates are Grade D · Use with caution: menu ingredients plus assumed portions and generic USDA foods. This grade describes evidence quality, not healthfulness. No portions were weighed, and no official Beaches calorie declaration was used.

Calories are summed from assumed edible grams × USDA calories per 100 g. Low/high scenarios usually use 65% and 150% of each central component weight; specified steak and recipe scenarios use their own bounds. They are not confidence intervals or guaranteed limits. Actual serving sizes, cooking fat, recipes and substitutions can put a dish outside the range. Values are rounded to 10 kcal; tiny unsweetened drinks display as <10 kcal.

Read the serving line: pizza means one assumed whole individual pizza, sushi rolls mean the listed four pieces, and the 14 oz steak for two is estimated per person. Optional add-ons and selectable sides are excluded unless explicitly included. Kimonos grill entries are separate meal components. Buffet visits, extra orders and refillable drinks have no fixed meal total.

These estimates use the saved menu descriptions, including older PDFs and guest reports. Only representative official menus and cocktail recipes were rechecked for this pass; this is not a full current-menu refresh. Photos were not used to infer weight or calories. Missing recipes, conflicting sources and grouped choices remain unavailable.

USDA FNDDS 2021–2023 supplies most food proxies; USDA SR28, page 678 supplies cooked conch. Each estimate lists its exact proxies and gram assumptions.
